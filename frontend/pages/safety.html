The 7 Factors

What goes into
your safety score.

Seven carefully weighted signals, analysed in under 300ms, to produce a single reliable score you can trust.

01
🔥
Peak-Hour Congestion
12 months of historical traffic pattern analysis to predict exact congestion on your route at the time of travel.
02
⚠️
Accident Zone Proximity
Distance-weighted scoring from government accident records. Zones with higher incident frequency penalise the route score.
03
🌧️
Weather Impact
Live integration with OpenWeatherMap. Rain, fog, and storm conditions apply dynamic safety penalties in real-time.
04
🛣️
Road Surface Quality
Crowdsourced and government road quality ratings flagging pothole zones, damaged surfaces, and unmaintained stretches.
05
🚧
Construction Diversions
Active construction zones sourced from municipal APIs, with automatic rerouting to avoid unexpected delays.
06
🚦
Intersection Density
Routes with higher intersection and signal counts are flagged as stop-heavy, giving users a complete picture of the journey.
07
🌙
Night Visibility Index
Street lighting density and night-time safety scoring, especially relevant for late-evening and early-morning rides.
