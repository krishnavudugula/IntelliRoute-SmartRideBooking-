IntelliRoute was founded with a single mission — make every urban journey in India measurably safer and smarter through the power of real-time data intelligence.

Our Mission

Safer roads for
every Indian.

We started IntelliRoute after realising that most ride-booking platforms only optimise for speed or price — ignoring the safety data that could genuinely protect riders. In India, 1.5 lakh road accident deaths happen every year. We believe technology can change that.

By fusing live traffic data with historical accident records, weather feeds, and road quality scores, we give every commuter the intelligence to make a safer journey — every single time.
